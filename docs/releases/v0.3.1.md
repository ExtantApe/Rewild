A small fix to `/rewild why`.

## Fixed

- **A chunk Rewild can't judge now says what keeps it.** A chunk generated before Rewild started tracking is unknown and never rewilds. `/rewild why` used to stop at "unknown". It now also lists what would keep the chunk anyway, such as a Waystation station or a claim.

## Upgrading

Replace the jar. No config or data changes.

## Tested

Unit tests, plus bots on an APFS copy of a real server on Paper 26.2 and 26.3 build 249, with Rewild on as a dry run on every survival world. On 26.3 a fresh chunk with 22 placed blocks was reported as "would rewild; kept blocks stay as they are", and nothing placed was lost. After the move to 26.3 Rewild paused resets on the older worlds and asked for `/rewild approve generator`, as designed. Not yet seen in a real game client.
