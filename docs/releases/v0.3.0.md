A redesign. Rewilding now works block by block: a chunk that's mined out and abandoned goes back to fresh terrain from the world's seed, and everything players placed stays exactly where it is. Before, a player's chest or a small build kept the whole chunk, so a quarry next to a house never filled in. Now the quarry fills in and the house stays.

**What stays in place:** every block a player placed, containers players filled, dirt paths (and any block types in the new `keep-blocks` list), areas kept with `/rewild keep` or by a plugin you respect, and the block holding each of those up. Claims, spawn, respawn points, named pets, nearby players, strongholds and `/rewild protect` still keep whole chunks.

**New:**
- `/rewild plugins`: on first start Rewild lists your plugins and resets nothing until you've said which to respect. A plugin installed later stops resets again until you answer for it.
- `/rewild keep` and `/rewild unkeep`: keep a block or a box of blocks, for yourself or on a plugin's behalf (`plugin:<Name>`). `--json` on `keep list` and `plugins`.
- `KeepProvider.areas()`: plugins can report block areas as well as whole chunks.
- Approval now generates fresh terrain for every chunk in the run, not just below-ground repairs.

**Upgrading from 0.2.x:**
- 0.2.x recorded only some placed blocks, so chunks it tracked stay unknown and never rewild. Only land generated after the upgrade can.
- The `intent` settings do nothing now and can be deleted.
- `/wr`, `/wildernessregen` and `wildernessregen.admin` are gone, as announced. Use `/rewild` and `rewild.admin`.
- Answer `/rewild plugins` before anything can reset.

**Tested** on throwaway Paper 26.3 (build 249) and 26.2 servers with a bot (see the README). Not yet seen in a real game client, and not run on 26.1.2.
