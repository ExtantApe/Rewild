Explosion holes heal on their own, gradually (D224), and Rewild keeps the Magic plugin's blocks.

## What's new

- A hole a creeper or TNT blows in natural land heals over about a week with no approval: the ground fills bottom up, about a step a day, then grass, then saplings, young trees and full trees, then flowers. It only heals when nothing a player placed is in it or within 8 blocks, and it waits while a player is within 24 blocks. Other holes wait for an admin's approved run as before.
- `/rewild healing` lists holes healing now. `step <id>` runs the next step, and `cancel <id>` stops one.
- The Magic plugin's blocks, automata and pending spell changes are kept in place. In worlds listed in `magic.worlds`, nothing resets or heals unless Magic is running.

## Upgrading

Replace the jar. New settings (`healing`, `magic`) get their defaults; add them to config.yml to change them. Healing is on by default, and while `dry-run` is true it only reports in the log.

## Tested

- Unit tests pass (103).
- `test/heal.mjs` on a throwaway Paper 26.3 server with a bot, 19 of 19: a TNT hole in fresh plains healed in 6 steps, filling from the bottom, with every one of its blocks back as it was (111 and 115 in two runs); a step waited while the bot stood near; a block set in the hole since was left alone; holes next to the bot's build and through a chest didn't heal on their own; dry run recorded and reported without placing; a healing survived a restart.
- Not yet tested: the Magic hook against a running Magic (no Magic jar in the test server), claims, and how healing looks in a real game client.
